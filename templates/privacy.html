Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app (not linked to your identity).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service:

  • To display your expenses and generate summaries.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To identify and fix bugs or performance issues.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us, after which it will be permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) to operate the app, but these providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only as directed by us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information provided in the app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
